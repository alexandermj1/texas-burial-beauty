# Modern Floral Opening Animation

## Goal
Replace the sparse first-load screen with a polished, colorful Texas Cemetery Brokers opening that feels modern and unmistakably on-brand.

## Changes
- Remove the gradient glow and cemetery-marker drawing.
- Build a clean parchment composition framed by the existing hibiscus, pink blossom, and sage-green botanical artwork.
- Reveal the florals as two cohesive corner arrangements rather than scattered decorations.
- Stage the company name with a crisp editorial reveal, a restrained hibiscus accent, and “Serving all of Texas.”
- Finish with a smooth floral-curtain handoff into the existing video home page.
- Keep the animation home-page-only, brief, accessible, and reduced-motion friendly.

## Verification
- Check the key reveal and final handoff at desktop and mobile sizes.
- Confirm text remains fully readable, artwork does not overlap it, and the page loads without errors.
